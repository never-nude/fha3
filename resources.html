Good to know

  • Trash, recycling & city services — pickup schedules and service requests live on the City of White Plains website, under the Public Works department.
  • Getting downtown — the Metro-North station, the library, and City Center all sit just beyond the neighborhood's northeast corner, within walking distance.
  • The hospital — White Plains Hospital and its emergency department sit just past Post Road, on the neighborhood's northeast edge.
In an emergency, call 911. For non-emergency city services, start at the City of White Plains website.
